## Contractor cancel / refund

| Case | Rule |
|---|---|
| A. Cancel before deposit | No money movement |
| B. Customer cancels after a valid deposit | Hold 15%. Do **not** auto-payout. Wait for verification. Proposed split (PENDING_ADVISOR_CONFIRMATION): BuildMate 5% of quote, contractor 10% of quote, only after terms were accepted, reason verified, and no open dispute |
| C. Contractor cancels after deposit | Mock-refund the full 15% to the customer. Do not post the 5% fee. Contractor does not receive the 10% |
| D. Mutual cancel | Record a refund ≤ available escrow balance; admin confirms |
| E. Dispute | FROZEN. No payout, no automatic refund |

The same escrow cannot be refunded and paid out for the same money. RELEASED is terminal.

Legal review is required before using forfeit language in real transactions.
